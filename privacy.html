Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Anwar Studio Official

The short version. EZ File Server does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. We run no servers and receive nothing from your headset.

When you start the file server, other devices on your own local network can reach the folder you chose. Files go directly between your devices — never through us or any cloud service.

1. Who we are

EZ File Server ("the app") is developed by Anwar Studio Official. Questions about this policy or your privacy: psopala93@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no backend, no database, no user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We do not receive your files, your file names, your IP address or any usage information.

3. How the file server works

The app's purpose is to run a file server on your headset (HTTP, WebDAV or FTP) so that your other devices can transfer files to and from it.

  • The server runs only while you have started it, and stops when you press Stop Server or close the app.
  • It listens on your local network. Anyone on that network who knows the address can reach the shared folder unless you turn on Require authentication.
  • HTTP, WebDAV and FTP are not encrypted. Use the server on a network you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi, and enable a password if other people share that network.
  • The username and password you enter are kept in memory only while the app runs. They are not saved to disk and are never sent anywhere except as the login check performed by the server on your headset.
  • While a transfer runs, the app shows its file name, size, speed and progress on screen. This information stays on the headset and is discarded shortly after the transfer ends.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy the app needs it
All files accessTo share the folder you choose (by default the headset's main storage) with your other devices and to save the files they upload.
InternetRequired by Android to open the server's network socket on your local network. The app does not contact any internet service.
Network stateTo read the headset's local IP address and show it as the connection address and QR code.

5. QR codes

The QR code only contains the server's local address (for example http://192.168.1.50:8080). If you choose Save to Downloads, it is saved as an image in your headset's Downloads folder. If you choose Share QR code, it is handed to the app you pick in the Android share sheet. Nothing is sent to us.

6. Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Your use of the Meta Horizon Store and Meta Quest is governed by Meta's own privacy policy.

7. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the headset, except files you saved yourself (such as a QR code in Downloads or files uploaded to your shared folder).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
